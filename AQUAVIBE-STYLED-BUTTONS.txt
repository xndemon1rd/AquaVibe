AquaVibe styled buttons
========================

All existing InlineKeyboardButton call-sites now use the AquaVibe styled-button
compatibility layer. Buttons are automatically assigned Telegram-native styles:
- success (green): normal actions
- primary (blue): navigation/info/support/open actions
- danger (red): close/cancel/delete/remove/stop actions

The compatibility layer uses Telegram's native MTProto KeyboardButtonStyle when
supported by the installed Pyrofork schema, and gracefully falls back to normal
buttons on older schemas.

Telegram documents these native styles as primary, success and danger.
